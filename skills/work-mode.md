# Work mode skills

## 1) Plan top 3

**Intent:** Focus on the highest-impact work.

**Steps:**
1. List all tasks for today.
2. Pick the top 3 most important outcomes.
3. Block time for each outcome.

**Done criteria:** Top 3 outcomes are clear and scheduled.

## 2) Start focus

**Intent:** Enter deep-work mode quickly.

**Steps:**
1. Close unrelated tabs and apps.
2. Silence notifications.
3. Start a 25-50 minute focus block.

**Done criteria:** One uninterrupted focus block is completed.

## 3) Communicate early

**Intent:** Reduce blockers and surprises.

**Steps:**
1. Share progress and blockers in team channel.
2. Ask for help early when blocked.
3. Confirm expectations for priority tasks.

**Done criteria:** Stakeholders have a clear status update.

## 4) Handoff clearly

**Intent:** Keep work moving across collaborators.

**Steps:**
1. Record current status.
2. Note next step and owner.
3. Include links to relevant files/issues.

**Done criteria:** Another teammate can continue without extra context.

## 5) Close the day

**Intent:** Improve continuity and planning.

**Steps:**
1. Mark completed work.
2. Capture lessons and remaining blockers.
3. Draft tomorrow's top 3.

**Done criteria:** Tomorrow starts with a clear plan.
